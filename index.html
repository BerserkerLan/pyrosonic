How it works

Everything below happens on your device in a few seconds.

  1. 1
    ListenThousands of short spectra measure bass, mids, treble, brightness and onsets; a dynamic-programming beat tracker locks onto the tempo and bar lines.
  2. 2
    UnderstandChroma features find the key and chords; a self-similarity novelty curve splits the song into intro, verses, builds, drops and finale.
  3. 3
    Read the wordsSynced lyrics (embedded, an .lrc file, or the open LRCLIB database) turn words like “love”, “fire” or “gold” into matching shells.
  4. 4
    ChoreographEach musical moment gets the right effect — peonies on downbeats, crossettes on snares, glitter on hi-hats, willows for long notes, salutes on drops — timed so it bursts on the beat.
  5. 5
    FireA GPU particle engine renders every star’s physics live, synced to the audio clock with latency compensation.

Sensitive to flashing light? Turn on Reduce flashing in the show settings for softer bursts and no strobes.
